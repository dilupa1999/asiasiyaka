Pack with Confidence, Style and Safe

Asia Siyaka Commodities PLC joined hands with M S H Packaging (Pvt) Ltd and started a new project in year 2023, keeping align with the goal of upgrading the service and diversifying the businesses we have done in the past.

Becoming a part of another key area of the tea value chain being a primary goal, it is our intention to make sure that our clients are benefitted through this project.

As an initial step, we are now supplying SLS certified Rigid Multiwall Paper sacks to tea manufacturers at a very competitive price and helping them in a way to reduce their COP.

Services we offer

  • Confirming Paper sacks orders at the best possible price whilst ensuring all parties are benefitted.
  • Door Step Deliveries without any additional cost.
  • Credit facility up to 2 months. (Terms and conditions apply).
  • Replacement for any damage bags. (Terms and conditions apply).
  • Holding inventory at the request of the client.
