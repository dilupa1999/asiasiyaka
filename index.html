About Asia Siyaka

Sri Lanka's Leading
Commodity Broker

Asia Siyaka is a leading commodity broker in Sri Lanka that started its operations in 1998. With 25 years of solid experience, the company has expanded its services across the value chain, including warehousing, advisory, and research support. Asia Siyaka is also a leading tea broker in Sri Lanka, handling a substantial 15% of the country's total traded tea volumes over the past 25 years.
